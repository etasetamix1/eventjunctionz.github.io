Itinerary

Day by day in the hills

Day 1

Arrival & mountain magic

  • Scenic arrival — Pickup from NJP or Bagdogra, then a drive through tea gardens and mountain roads.
  • Malli river rafting — Feel the rush of the rapids before you’ve even checked in.
  • Bonfire & BBQ — Settle into your mountain-view resort in Bara Mangwa with local cuisine and group games under the stars.

Day 2

Adrenaline & wilderness

  • Sunrise breakfast — Panoramic mountain views as the Himalayas light up in gold.
  • Paragliding / hiking / village visit — Soar above Kalimpong on a tandem flight, or explore on foot.
  • Evening in Darjeeling — Transfer to your hotel; campfire and stargazing.

Day 3

River adventure & village culture

  • Mountain biking — Ride from Darjeeling to Bijan Bari on MTB cycles. Backup vehicle available if you tire.
  • Village & tea garden walk — Explore local villages and iconic tea estates.
  • Stay at Bijan Bari — A relaxed evening with the mountains as your backdrop.

Day 4

Sunrise, memories & farewell

  • Morning photography walk — Capture the Himalayas in early light and mist.
  • Breakfast & checkout — A final hearty meal before you pack up the memories.
  • Optional Mirik — Stop at Mirik Lake or scenic viewpoints on the way back.
  • Return transfer — Drop-off at NJP Railway Station or Bagdogra Airport.

FAQ

Darjeeling adventure package FAQs

What is included in The Ultimate Darjeeling Adventure?

3 nights stay (Bara Mangwa resort, Darjeeling riverside camp, Bijan Bari resort), all meals, NJP/Bagdogra transfers, local transport, adventure coordination, tour coordinator, first aid, bonfire & BBQ, optional Mirik stop.

Which adventures are part of the trip?

Paragliding over Kalimpong, mountain biking Darjeeling to Bijan Bari, Malli river rafting, guided trekking, campfire sessions, and nature / photography walks.

Who is this Darjeeling tour ideal for?

Corporate groups, friends and couples, student groups, and team outings looking for a 4-day Himalayan adventure in West Bengal.
